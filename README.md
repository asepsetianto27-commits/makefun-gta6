> Rebuilt from a clean session — fresh write-up of the same game.

Welcome to Solmara, a fictional coastal city that never really sleeps. In **Solmara Nights** you play Jax Marlow, a driver-for-hire who owes a debt to the fixer Mama Rosa, and the only way out is through the streets: four story missions, fares on the side, and the police never far behind.

This is a 2D top-down open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, made to run in a desktop browser from plain static files — no libraries, no network requests, all art drawn in code and all sound synthesised with WebAudio.

## What is inside

- A living city to roam on foot or behind the wheel — roads, blocks, buildings, beachfront, parks and bridges.
- Traffic that keeps to its lanes, pedestrians on the sidewalks, and cars you can steal (walk up and press E).
- A wanted system: commit a crime and the stars climb; police cars and officers chase, ram and try to arrest you. Lay low and the heat fades.
- Combat on foot — fists, plus a pistol with mouse aim once you pick one up — while armed police and pedestrians can shoot back.
- Four story missions: a timed first fare, a stolen-car delivery, surviving a 3-star wanted level, and a final package drop under pursuit. Taxi fares, cash, reputation and a localStorage save system round it out.
- Full HUD with health, cash, wanted stars, mission objective and distance, clock and speed, plus a live minimap showing you, cars, the mission marker and the police.
- Day and dusk lighting, headlights after dark, skid marks, smoke, muzzle flash, screen shake, and a procedural music loop.

## Controls

WASD or arrow keys to move and drive · E to enter or exit a car · Space for the handbrake · Shift to run · mouse to aim, click to shoot · F to punch · M for the mission list · Esc to pause.

## How to run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open `http://localhost:8080` in desktop Chrome and hit New Game (or Continue if you have a save).
